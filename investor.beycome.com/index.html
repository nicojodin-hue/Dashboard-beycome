Executive Summary

Overview

Revenue was $274,778 in April, up 11.7% from $245,911 in March — the highest monthly revenue on record. The growth was driven by Title revenue, which more than doubled MoM to $77,086 from $37,780 (+104%). Basic Listing ($108,851, +2.6%) and Enhanced Listing ($46,683, +1.0%) were broadly stable. Concierge declined to $14,342 (–41.2%). Buy Side recovered to $11,400 (+14.0%).

Listing client volume was 1,203 unique clients in April (1,069 Basic, 115 Enhanced, 19 Concierge). Refund activity was $11,842 (78 transactions), above the Q1 average of ~$8,500/month and ~66 transactions/month. Active users reached 296,000 per Google Analytics, up from 134,000 in March.

Gross margin compressed to 80% from 85% in March. Title COGS scaled with the higher volume ($23,256 vs. $9,778), and Sell Side COGS increased to $28,508 (driven by Photography at $21,570). Net loss was ($46,969), representing a –17% net margin, an improvement from March (–22%).

Total S&M was $114,002, up 10.6% from March. Traffic spend (Advertising + Acquisition) was $72,013 with a Traffic CAC of $60 per listing client, flat versus March ($60). Total CAC (all S&M / listing clients) was $95, up from $85 in March. Revenue per client increased to $228 from $203 (+12.4%), keeping the revenue-to-CAC ratio at 2.4x.

Cash on hand was $1,884,292 across all entities as of end of April. Estimated runway is 31.6 months based on the trailing 4-month average burn of $59,666.
