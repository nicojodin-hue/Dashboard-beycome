How Long Is Mortgage Pre-Approval Good For?

Updated April 13, 2026 · 4 min read

Most mortgage pre-approval letters expire in 60 to 90 days. If you are not under contract before the clock runs out, you will need to reapply — but with the right timing strategy, that is easy to avoid.

Standard Pre-Approval Validity Window

Most mortgage pre-approval letters are valid for 60 to 90 days from the date the lender issues the letter. The exact duration varies by lender — some issue 30-day letters, while others go as long as 120 days. The expiration date is stated on the letter itself.

After a pre-approval expires, the lender considers your financial information stale and will require a full re-application — updated documents, a fresh credit pull, and a new underwriting review — before issuing a new letter.

Why Do Pre-Approvals Expire?

Lenders set expiration dates because your financial picture can change quickly. Income, debt, credit scores, and interest rates all shift over time. A letter issued three months ago may no longer reflect your current borrowing power.

Key Factors That Can Change

  • Credit score changes from new accounts or missed payments
  • Job changes or income fluctuations
  • New debt (car loans, credit cards, personal loans)
  • Interest rate movements affecting your qualifying amount
  • Changes in savings or down payment funds

How to Extend or Renew Your Pre-Approval

If your pre-approval is about to expire and you haven't found a home yet, contact your lender to request a renewal. In most cases, the process is faster than the original application because the lender already has your base documentation on file.

Pro tip: Start your home search within the first two weeks of getting pre-approved to maximize your window.

Pre-Approval vs. Pre-Qualification

Feature Pre-Qualification Pre-Approval
Credit check Soft pull (or none) Hard pull
Documentation Self-reported Verified
Validity No formal expiration 60-90 days
Strength Informal estimate Conditional commitment

Bottom Line

A mortgage pre-approval typically lasts 60 to 90 days. Plan your home search timeline accordingly, and keep your lender informed if you need a renewal. Having an active pre-approval letter strengthens your offer and shows sellers you're a serious buyer.
